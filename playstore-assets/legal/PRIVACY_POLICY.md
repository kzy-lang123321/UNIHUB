## 2. Information We Collect

### A. Personal Information Provided Directly by You
* **Account Information:** Email address, full name, username, password, phone number (optional).
* **Profile Information:** Profile avatar image, profile cover/banner image, short bio, institution/university name, department, degree level, field of study, academic interests, and external social/portfolio links.
* **User-Generated Content (Community Library):** Uploaded educational materials (PDF documents, books, lecture notes), book metadata (title, author, subject, category, description), and cover thumbnails.

### B. Device & Technical Information
* **Authentication & Biometrics:** If enabled, your device's biometric authentication (fingerprint / face ID) is processed entirely locally on your device hardware using standard Android biometric APIs (`expo-local-authentication`). We do not store or transmit raw biometric data to our servers.
* **App Performance & Device Identifiers:** Non-identifying technical data, app version, operating system version, and crash logs to maintain app stability.

---

## 3. How We Use Your Information
We use the information collected for the following purposes:
1. **To Provide and Maintain Our Services:** Enabling you to discover, read, and download university lecture notes and community-uploaded books.
2. **Profile Management:** Displaying your public profile, academic background, and contributions to fellow students (if your profile visibility is set to public).
3. **Content Moderation & Copyright Compliance:** Reviewing community-uploaded books in a pending-review moderation queue prior to public release to prevent copyright infringement or inappropriate content.
4. **Offline Access:** Storing cached books, user profile details, and library metadata locally on your device for offline reading.
5. **Account Security:** Authenticating logins, supporting password resets, and preventing unauthorized account access.

---

## 4. Third-Party Services
UniHub integrates with trusted industry-standard third-party service providers:
* **Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage):** For secure cloud authentication, user account management, and storage of approved community educational materials.
* **Expo Framework:** Provides device API bridges and core runtime services.

These third parties process data in compliance with strict industry data security standards (SOC 2, ISO 27001).
