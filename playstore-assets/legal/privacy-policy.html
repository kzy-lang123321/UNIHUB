Privacy Policy for UniHub

Effective Date: November 23, 2026 | App: UniHub (com.megatech.unihub)

1. Introduction

Welcome to UniHub. We are committed to protecting the privacy of university students and users. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, handle, and protect your information when using our mobile application.

2. Data We Collect

  • Account & Profile: Name, student email address, username, bio, academic department, university name, interests, and profile pictures.
  • Educational Contributions: PDF books, lecture notes, titles, descriptions, and cover images contributed to the Community Library.
  • Device Security: Device-level biometric authentication (fingerprint/face recognition) managed strictly on-device via Android OS APIs.

3. How We Use Data

  • To display academic resources and student profiles.
  • To moderate community uploads through an administrative review pipeline before public discovery.
  • To cache educational materials locally for offline reading.

4. Third-Party Services

UniHub utilizes Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Storage) for secure cloud database operations. We do not sell your personal data to any advertisers or third-party brokers.
