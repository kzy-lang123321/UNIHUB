### Full Description (4000 characters max)
```markdown
UniHub is the ultimate academic platform built specifically for university and college students. Whether you need lecture notes for upcoming exams, want to read textbooks offline, or wish to share your study materials with fellow students, UniHub has you covered!

📚 KEY FEATURES:

1. COMPREHENSIVE LECTURE NOTES & MATERIALS
Access categorized lecture notes, slides, and study resources organized by university department, semester, and course. Save materials locally to study offline anytime, anywhere.

2. COMMUNITY BOOK LIBRARY
Contribute to the student community! Upload educational materials, reference books, and class guides in PDF format. Add custom covers, categorize by subject and education level, and help fellow students excel.

3. QUALITY & COPYRIGHT ASSURED
All community-uploaded books go through a transparent review queue to ensure high educational quality, appropriate content, and strict copyright compliance before becoming public.

4. PERSONALIZED STUDENT PROFILES
Manage your academic identity with customizable profile pictures, cover banners, bio, university affiliation, degree level, and interests. Showcase your uploaded contributions to your classmates.

5. FAST SEARCH & SMART FILTERS
Instantly locate what you need by searching across book titles, authors, course subjects, departments, and education levels.

6. OFFLINE-FIRST ARCHITECTURE
Designed with modern caching technology so you can access your saved materials and read books without consuming mobile data.

7. SECURE & PRIVATE
Enjoy biometric security (fingerprint / face unlock) and privacy controls over your public profile visibility.

---
Empower your campus learning with UniHub. Download today and join the community of collaborative students!
```
